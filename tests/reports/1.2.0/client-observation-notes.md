# Native client observation notes

Supplemental native-client queue: `tests/results/full-fights/2026-11-05T02-22-16.715609400Z` (UTC). Test-helper SHA-256: `2CA3BA0D197C345B5CE1CFABD22833BB1EE2A22AD8BCC9EA39960D9662B78EF0`.

These are supplemental automated native-client attempts, not human playthroughs. HP and damage are the primary measurements. Boss AI, attack selection, movement, protections and phase transitions remain native. A time limit or knocked defeat does not establish victory or natural coverage of every phase; controlled attack/phase regressions supply the separate numerical coverage.

Each encounter starts with 20 HP. Diamond uses plain diamond armor, diamond sword and plain bow. Netherite uses Protection IV netherite armor, netherite sword and Power V bow. Both have a shield, ordinary cooked beef and arrows. Consequently these attempts compare complete loadouts, not armor alone; use the separate armor matrix for armor-only comparisons. There are no mid-fight HP, cooldown or phase resets. Completed native knocked defeats may be followed by an administrative kill of only the test player to finish the between-encounter bleedout; the loss is preserved.

Terminology: the machine outcome `lost_downed` is a lethal **knocked** defeat handled by RagRevival, requiring revival or bleedout. The recorded native lethal event establishes this outcome; the final snapshot need not remain at exactly 1 HP. A **nonlethal down/ragdoll** from Sable is different: the ordinary crouch action can get up after the native delay. Native crouch recovery is not a revival and does not restore HP.

The isolated server uses the source server's view distance 12 and simulation distance 9; the client uses 12/9. Encounters use prepared flat arenas, a water arena for Leviathan, and native End terrain for the dragon; they do not recreate every natural structure or environmental mechanic. The test client removes FlightLib's proven development-only diamond/gold-chestplate jetpack caster so ordinary armor behaves as it does in production. Production equipment/accessory flight providers remain. The source server folder is read-only. Earlier hover-affected runs and the earlier 32-block tracking-limited Lich attempt are excluded from this queue's evidence.

## Measurements and interpretation

Damage values below are HP, not hearts. Native event totals are not net HP lost because food healing, shield blocks, hurt cooldowns, native protections and death replacement affect the result. Incoming sources such as fall, freeze and wall collision are kept separate from directly attributed boss attacks.

The client records actual received boss-event names and progress. A finite progress value in `[0,1]` confirms the received value, not every aspect of custom rendering; screenshots separately check the visible HUD. Native shared or protected-phase bars need not equal the nearest individual boss's HP fraction.

Shield-use requests are not successful blocks. The server's `isBlocking` observations and shield durability supply stronger evidence. One-second samples can miss brief blocking windows. Corpse/ragdoll entities from earlier native deaths can remain visible in the reused arena; their visibility alone does not establish a collision obstruction.

## Final telemetry audit

The 46 bounded attempts contain 28 lethal/knocked defeats, 17 time limits and one native boss defeat (Protection IV netherite Minoshroom). Diamond had 16 defeats and 7 time limits; netherite had 12 defeats, 10 time limits and the one victory. Recorded encounter time totals 4586.6 game seconds. These outcome counts include the explicit fixture/controller limitations above and are not a skill or armor-only success-rate benchmark.

All 23 boss maximum-HP pairs were identical across the two loadouts; all player maximum-HP observations were 20. The 18,075 matched client records produced no JSON parse failures, controller exceptions, overlay-observation errors, invalid received bar values or missing-chunk samples. There were 8,929 received progress observations across 47 native bar-event IDs, all within `[0,1]`. Only the two Warden cases lacked named boss bars, as expected. This validates the recorded values and tracking observations; separate screenshots and controlled native-bar checks address rendering and specific lifecycle semantics.

Native blocking was sampled 41 times, and shield durability loss corroborated absorbed damage in 10 encounters. The 1,340 shield-input requests are not counted as 1,340 blocks. Observed net steak use before lethal inventory changes was 69; natural food healing remained enabled. The source modpack's extra wall collisions, status damage, nonlethal ragdolls and true knockout replacement remained active and were distinguished from directly attributed boss hits.

All 46 attempts are retained in the [compact encounter export](client-encounters/fight-summary.md), with [encounter rows](client-encounters/encounters.csv), [server timeline](client-encounters/server-timeline.csv), [client timeline](client-encounters/client-timeline.csv) and [received bar observations](client-encounters/client-bars.csv). Unsuccessful, unfinished and limited observations are not converted into passing full-fight claims.
