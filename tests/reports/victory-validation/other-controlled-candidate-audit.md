Knight CHARGE_MINUSZ has a narrower test of its native formation setter, with separate native weapon-damage tests. Its complete movement path remains unobserved. The raw natural-catalogue gaps stay visible and no additional victory is inferred.

The [machine-readable audit](other-controlled-candidate-audit.json) includes exact scenarios, assertions and source hashes.
