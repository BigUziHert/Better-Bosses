## Explicit budget-exhaustion stress

12 additional scenarios retained player health, equipment/durability and each projectile ledger while repeating native collision callbacks after clearing only hurt cooldown. They independently checked accepted damage against the eight-heart reference budget, verified final-hit truncation, then checked that another callback caused no material health loss or budget spend. The same held projectile object was deliberately reused after native discard; these are adversarial compatibility checks, not ordinary gameplay collisions.

Half also simulate a compatibility mod removing immediate impact/splash invulnerability: a scoped test listener clears only the fixture victim cooldown when the native explosion starts. The native Ur-Ghast power4 callback then records accepted 4.5 + 3.5 = 8 diamond hearts, or 1.451684 + 1.139647 = 2.591331 hearts in Protection IV netherite. Harbinger missile and homing-missile variants also exhaust their own retained budgets across repeated callbacks. No damage source, ownership, player health or explosion calculation is replaced.

[All budget-exhaustion measurements](compound-budget-exhaustion.csv) preserve both armor profiles. Repeated callbacks can split the same reference budget across more than two accepted hits; their total netherite damage is therefore distinct from the two-component bound. The generic two-component bound is 2.592 hearts; Harbinger impact at most 2.25 gives the tighter bound 2.583413. Numeric-zero exhaustion allows at most 0.00001 HP/reference-heart rounding remainder.
