## Resonarium durability

The installed DeeperDarker 1.4.1 handler subtracts one quarter of post-mitigation damage for each Resonarium piece when the source does not bypass armor, and damages those items. Its armor cannot accept Protection enchantments. Full intact armor can therefore prevent a hit from costing health while the gear wears out. Armor-bypassing magic and Warden sonic damage remain dangerous. The following native Warden melee probes keep the same armor across 12 hits, resetting only hit cooldown. The measuring player has extra health to retain later outcomes after gear breaks.
